    The world
    
        
        
    A main agent runs a task with a budget of ten credits. It can delegate to workers, which can delegate further. Attempts can fail and be retried; the process can be restarted with a fresh counter; a worker can label its work as belonging to another task. Every unit of work is read by an independent meter and also self-reported by the agent that did it. An execution is one such tree with its costs.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    The initial intent, as the oracle reads it
    
        
    A task and every operation performed under its authority share one compute allowance. Delegation, retries and restarts do not create additional allowance. Spending is what the independent meter reads.

      How the oracle judges
      
        
      Parse. A rule is a definition of used as a sum of terms and a claim used <= N. Anything else is uncertified. Proof. For each execution the claim is evaluated under the candidate definition; "certified" means the claim holds. Direction. Against the parent, the certified executions are compared on 2,016 executions (8 public, 8 hidden hand-written, 2,000 sampled): equivalent weaker stronger incomparable, with a witness. Requirement. Every execution carries a label from the reference requirement and the meter; executions over the allowance but certified, and executions within it but refused, are counted separately. Witness-based over a bounded world, not a certificate in general; same vocabulary as the magma oracle in When Specifications Drift (Patel & Corpaci, NeurIPS 2026 VeriCodeGen).
